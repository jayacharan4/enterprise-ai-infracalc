How to Use This Tool

1. Start with GenAI Sizing — select a model family and size, configure your workload parameters (concurrent users, token lengths, RPS), choose a GPU type, and review the estimated GPU count, memory requirements, and performance metrics.

2. Move to TCO Calculator — sizing outputs auto-populate the TCO inputs. Add on-premises hardware assumptions (server cost, power, facility) and compare against cloud IaaS hourly rates. Set your analysis period (1–5 years) and review cost breakdown, break-even timing, and savings percentage.

3. Review Results — the Results page consolidates your sizing and TCO findings into an executive summary with key assumptions flagged. Use the Export PDF button to generate a print-ready report.

4. Adjust Assumptions — all major assumptions are editable. Visit Formulas & Assumptions to understand the calculation methodology and override defaults where you have better data.
⚠️ Planning Estimate Disclaimer: All calculations in this tool are for planning and directional guidance only. Results are estimates based on published hardware specifications, publicly available model parameters, and industry reference benchmarks. Actual deployment performance, costs, and requirements will vary based on your specific workload characteristics, vendor configurations, negotiated pricing, and operational conditions. This tool does not constitute a binding quote, vendor recommendation, or engineering guarantee. Validate all estimates with your infrastructure vendors before making procurement decisions.

Calculation Assumptions

Formulas and defaults used in this estimate

▲
Memory Formulas
[1] Model Weights Memory (GB) = Params_B × BytesPerParam [2] KV Cache Memory (GB) = 2 × NumLayers × NumKVHeads × HeadDim × EffectiveContextLen × ConcurrentReqs × KVBytesPerElement / 1e9 EffectiveContextLen = (InputTokens + OutputTokens) × ConversationDepth ConversationDepth = 1 (stateless) or avg turns in flight (multi-turn chat) [3] Total Required (GB) = (WeightsMem + KVMem) × (1 + OverheadFraction)
Performance Formulas
[4] Output TPS = GPU_MemBW_TBs × 1e12 / (2 × Params_B × 1e9) × EfficiencyFactor × NumGPUs [5] TTFT (ms) = InputTokens × 2 × Params_B × 1e9 / (GPU_BF16_TFLOPS × 1e12 × Efficiency × NumGPUs) × 1000 [6] TPOT (ms) = 1000 / (OutputTPS / ConcurrentReqs) [7] E2E Latency (ms) = TTFT + (OutputTokens × TPOT)
Efficiency Formula
[8] Tokens/sec/Watt = OutputTPS / (NumGPUs × GPU_TDP_W)
ℹ️ Formulas [4]–[7] use memory-bandwidth-bound decode assumptions. Efficiency factor (default 0.35) accounts for batching overhead and practical MFU. See Formulas & Assumptions page for full derivations.
